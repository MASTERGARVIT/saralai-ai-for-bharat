# SaralAI – Requirements Specification

## 1. Problem Definition

Citizens struggle to access and understand government schemes and public opportunities due to fragmented information sources, complex bureaucratic language, lack of regional-language support, and unclear eligibility criteria. As a result, many high-impact public programs remain underutilized.

---

## 2. Target Users

### Primary Users
- Students seeking scholarships, internships, and skilling programs
- Job seekers and early-career professionals
- Rural and semi-urban citizens accessing public services

### Secondary Users
- NGOs and community facilitators
- Government outreach teams (future scope)

---

## 3. User Pain Points

- Information spread across multiple portals
- Content written in complex and formal language
- No personalized eligibility guidance
- Lack of support for Indian languages
- Dependence on intermediaries or unofficial sources

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

### Core Features
- Multilingual text-based query support
- Voice-based interaction for low-literacy users
- Personalized eligibility-based scheme recommendations
- Simplified explanations of schemes and benefits
- Step-by-step guidance with official links

### Supporting Features
- Automatic language detection
- Follow-up clarification questions
- Mobile-first and low-bandwidth design

---

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

- High availability and scalability
- Secure handling of user inputs
- Low operational cost
- Fast response time (< 3 seconds)

---

## 6. Why AI Is Needed

Traditional rule-based or keyword-based systems cannot:
- Understand informal, mixed-language queries
- Reason dynamically about eligibility
- Personalize responses based on user context
- Summarize complex policy documents

AI enables:
- Natural language understanding
- Context-aware reasoning
- Multilingual accessibility
- Conversational interaction at scale

---

## 7. Success Metrics

- Accuracy of scheme recommendations
- Reduction in user confusion and follow-up queries
- User engagement and completion of guidance flows
- Language coverage and accessibility
